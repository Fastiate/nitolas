Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 26, 2026

This policy explains how the photo editing app "nitolas" (the "App") handles information.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect any personal information. There is no account registration, and the App does not use analytics, advertising, or tracking.

2. Your Photos

  • Photos are opened, edited, and saved entirely on your iPhone. The App never sends your photos or edits to the App's operator or any other company's servers.
  • Face and person detection runs only on your device using built-in iPhone features (Apple's Vision). The results are saved with the photo in your photo library as part of the edit data.
  • Access to your photo library is used to save edited photos and let you continue editing later. You can edit without allowing access.
  • A photo you choose for the "Match" feature (matching the look of a reference photo) is also analyzed only on your device. The App never stores or sends it, and it is discarded when you close the App.
  • So you can resume after the App closes unexpectedly, unsaved edits are kept temporarily inside the App as adjustment values only (never the photo itself). They are removed when you save or discard, and deleted together with the App.
  • Photos and edit data saved to your photo library are synced by Apple's iCloud if you use iCloud Photos.

3. Location

You can choose in Settings whether to keep or remove the location where a photo was taken when exporting or sharing. By default, location is removed when sharing, and kept when exporting to the Photos app, as in the original photo.

4. Purchases

Subscriptions are purchased and managed by Apple's App Store. The App never receives your payment information. Your subscription status is checked with records issued by Apple and stored on your device.

5. Sharing

When you share a photo, you choose where it goes (such as social media). How it is handled after that is governed by that service's policies.

6. Children

The App does not collect information from children.

7. Changes to This Policy

If we change this policy, we will let you know on this page and in the App.
