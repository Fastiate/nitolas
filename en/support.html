FAQ

Which iPhones are supported?

nitolas works on iPhones running iOS 26 or later.

Can I revert an edited photo?

Yes. Edits are saved in the Photos app. Open the photo in Photos, tap Edit, then Revert to restore the original. You can also open it again in nitolas to continue editing.

Are my photos sent anywhere?

No. Photos are opened, edited, and saved entirely on your iPhone. A photo you pick as a look reference is analyzed on your device and never stored. See the Privacy Policy for details.

What does Standard (subscription) include?

Standard includes 12 mood presets, 9 lipstick shades, and 3 text styles (film date, handwritten, minimal). Every other edit is free. Anyone can try Standard features; saving, exporting, and sharing photos that use them requires Standard.

How do I use Standard on another iPhone?

Tap Restore Purchases in the app's Settings while signed in with the Apple Account you used to purchase.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Tap Manage Subscription in the app's Settings, or open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions. The subscription renews automatically unless canceled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period.

Is location information kept in shared photos?

By default, location is removed when sharing. You can change this under Export and Share in the app's Settings.

How do I change the language?

nitolas supports English and Japanese. Open nitolas in the Settings app and choose Language.
